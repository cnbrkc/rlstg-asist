# Caption Prompt

Create a concise, engaging Turkish Instagram Reels caption from the supplied automotive news context.

Requirements:
- Do not invent facts.
- Keep the opening sentence attention-grabbing.
- Preserve exact model, brand, figures, dates, and claims from the verified context.
- Write naturally in Turkish.
- Add relevant hashtags at the end.
- Avoid generic filler and clickbait that changes the factual meaning.
